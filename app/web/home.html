GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

What does the hourly rate include?+

The selected studio, its listed basic lighting and backdrop setup, Wi-Fi, and the studio amenities shown above.

Can I extend my session?+

Yes, when the following slot is available. Additional time is charged at the applicable hourly rate.

How do I secure the slot?+

Complete the booking details and choose pay now or pay at the studio. Your confirmation reserves the selected time.

Can I bring a larger crew or special props?+

Check the space capacity and rules first. Contact the studio team for heavy props, food sets, smoke, pets, or unusual production needs.
